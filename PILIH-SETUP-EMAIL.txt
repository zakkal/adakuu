----------------------------------------------------------------
PILIHAN 1: GMAIL SMTP (Production Ready)
----------------------------------------------------------------
✅ Email masuk ke inbox REAL
✅ Gratis
❌ Setup agak ribet (perlu App Password)

Cara:
1. Buka: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Aktifkan 2-Factor Authentication
3. Buka: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
4. Generate App Password (16 karakter)
5. Jalankan: php setup-smtp-interactive.php
6. Paste App Password
7. DONE!

Panduan lengkap: SETUP-EMAIL-GMAIL.md

PILIHAN 2: MAILTRAP (Development/Testing) ⭐ RECOMMENDED
----------------------------------------------------------------
✅ Setup 5 menit
✅ Tidak perlu App Password
✅ Preview email di web dashboard
❌ Email tidak masuk inbox real (fake inbox)

Cara:
1. Daftar: https://mailtrap.io/register/signup
2. Login → Email Testing → My Inbox
3. Copy Username & Password dari SMTP Settings
4. Jalankan: php setup-mailtrap.php
5. Paste credentials
6. DONE!

Lihat email di: https://mailtrap.io/ (web dashboard)
